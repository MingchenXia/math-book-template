\chapter{Questions about continuous functions}
\label{chap:continuous-functions}
Fix a compact space $X$. The preceding compactness argument gives a way to control the image of a continuous real-valued function. The following demonstration questions record what remains to be written in this manuscript.
\BookProblem{OP-001}{Boundedness of a continuous function}{Is every continuous function $f\colon X\to\mathbb{R}$ bounded?}
\BookProblem{OP-002}{Existence of extrema}{If $X$ is nonempty, does every continuous function $f\colon X\to\mathbb{R}$ attain its maximum and minimum?}
