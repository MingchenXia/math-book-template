\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:OP-001}
Let $X$ be a compact topological space. Every continuous function $f\colon X\to\mathbb{R}$ is bounded.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If $X$ is empty, the assertion is vacuous. Otherwise the open sets $f^{-1}((-n,n))$, for positive integers $n$, cover $X$. Compactness gives a finite subcover. Let $N$ be the largest index in that subcover. Since these sets are nested, $X=f^{-1}((-N,N))$, so $|f(x)|<N$ for every $x\in X$.
\end{proof}
